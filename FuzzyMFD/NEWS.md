# FuzzyMFD 0.1.0

* First version: piecewise-linear fuzzy sets and membership function shapes;
  core, support, boundaries, height, normality, convexity, crossover points and
  prototype; exact union, intersection and complement; fuzzification of crisp
  readings; rank ordering; inductive reasoning by entropy minimisation with
  repeated partitioning; max-membership, centroid, weighted-average and
  mean-max defuzzification; the worked examples of Ross (2000).
